Installing

Phone says "blocked" or "unknown app"?

That is normal for apps outside the Play Store. Tap Settings on the pop-up → turn on Allow from this source → go back and tap Install.

Is it safe?

Yes. StatusDrop is open source, so anyone can check the code. It has no ads, no trackers and needs no account.

How do I update?

Open StatusDrop → Settings → Check for updates. Or download again from this page.

A video won't download?

Only public videos work. Private videos, or ones that need a login, can't be downloaded. The app tells you when that's the case.
